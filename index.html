About Champions

A Nigerian pharmaceutical institution built on heritage and evidence

Registered on 4 June 1993, Champions Pharmaceuticals came out of a family pharmaceutical heritage that spans generations, and set out to serve Nigeria's healthcare needs with regulated, documented, quality-assured medicine.

Our work today runs across three connected areas: regulated distribution, structured research and development, and public-health partnership. All of it is held to NAFDAC and Federal Ministry of Health standards.
